# Knovum 0.3.0

This release introduces selection export and multi-section selection on the canvas, adds Big LaMa inpainting and depth layer separation for image workflows, expands the local Whisper and VoxCPM2 speech models catalog, and moves derived content with its sources between Spaces.

Selections on the canvas can now be exported directly, making it easy to capture and share arbitrary arrangements of notes, media, and diagrams. Canvas workflows also support selecting and manipulating multiple sections together. When transferring content between Spaces, derived items and their source assets move together as a coherent unit so dependencies and citations remain intact.

Image editing gains high-fidelity local object removal and repair powered by the Big LaMa inpainting model. Depth layers are now separated from sticker sheets for cleaner multi-layer compositing, and image selection derivation strictly follows your configured Settings models.

Local speech capabilities are substantially expanded with new Whisper transcription models across Base, Medium, MediumFull, and Large-v3-Turbo variants, complete with quantized and floating-point precision options. Speech synthesis adds BF16 execution for VoxCPM2, and speaker diarization includes updated embedding models.

Background enrichment tasks in Task Center now pause and wait for missing model or environment setups instead of immediately failing work, and quick content previews on Windows open directly in your associated system application.
