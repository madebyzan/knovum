# Knovum 0.1.0

This release makes the workbench answer at the speed of its tabs, lets the video workbench read
your sources directly, and makes the application explain its AI readiness before it asks for your
patience.

Leaving a tab no longer marks it unsaved, and switching no longer waits behind a serial write of
every document you left open. An editability toggle is not an edit, and one autosave model serves
every editor, so a tab or Space switch commits nothing and rebuilds nothing. After a relaunch the
workbench reopens the tab strip it had when it closed, in order and with the active tab and every
Space those tabs referenced, instead of dropping you back to the Inbox. Tabs whose Space is gone
or whose content no longer accepts its editor do not return, and the Inbox stays.

A conversation keeps its shape: turns stay stable across a run, one conversation keeps one
runtime, and pinned desktop-agent adapters start from the local cache instead of a registry round
trip per answer. A media import that fails now leaves without cleanup errors behind it.

The video workbench reads your sources. Preview and timeline draw from the source media rather
than waiting on a proxy, audio decodes as a stream and waveform peaks arrive as they are read, so
a long clip's timeline fills in front of you instead of appearing later in one piece. Filmstrip
thumbnails decode at the lane's device-pixel height with one frame per slot, so nothing is
upscaled, repeated or stretched.

The application explains AI readiness before a feature runs. Every control states its requirement
up front, routed purpose, model choice or local capability, and one resolver decides readiness
against AI settings, the local model catalog and desktop-agent detection. A refusal names what is
missing and opens the Settings pane that fixes it; a local model that is not downloaded is named
with its size and prepared with progress in Tasks instead of failing silently.

A Section on the canvas can arrange its children itself: with auto-layout its children fill its
width in reading order and its height follows the rows, and Tidy up packs a selection into the
squarest table. Software update shows real progress, received and total size with the percentage
beneath it and an indeterminate sweep while the size is unknown or the signature is checked, and
renders its release notes as Markdown instead of raw text. The About pane no longer repeats its
own title, and names and directory layout across the repository follow the naming conventions.
