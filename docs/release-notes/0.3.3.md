# Knovum 0.3.3

This release rebuilds video dubbing around subtitle-accurate timing and measured delivery loudness, adds prompt-guided voice cloning with a default voice profile, and gives Task Center batch retry and dismissal. The canvas empty state and voice management also receive a desktop polish pass.

Video dubbing now translates lines as one batch and keeps the terms you list as preserved terms unchanged. Dialogue is split on natural word pauses so silence keeps the original background audio, and overlong lines are shortened by a speaking-rate-aware translation retry and duration-aware synthesis before any tempo compression, so speech starts on its subtitle time and is never cut mid-word. The final mix anchors dialogue at -17 LUFS over -23 LUFS beds and masters the program to -16 LUFS with a -1.5 dBTP ceiling, while the original music and effects are separated and ducked instead of discarded. Published videos carry the dubbed track as their primary audio track.

Voice profiles can be marked as the default and applied to every dubbing line at once, and capable models now accept prompt-guided reference cloning with an optional style description. Voice audition buffers preview chunks until generation completes, reports preparing and synthesizing progress, and no longer stutters word by word. The voice profile action menu works again inside settings overlays, uses tighter spacing, and states clearly what deleting a voice removes.

Task Center retries refresh their execution targets, support retrying all failed work at once, and let you dismiss tasks you no longer need. Media and visual enrichment can be reprocessed directly through retranscription, chapter and summary regeneration, and visual reanalysis, and truncated task metadata now reveals its full text on hover.

On the canvas, an empty Space presents an illustrated knowledge mascot with direct desktop actions, and audio playback stops when its node is deselected.
