# Knovum 0.3.6

This release puts the model decision at the moment you submit work and freezes it so a retry replays the same model, adds spoken delivery instructions to audiobooks and short dubs, makes canvas group selection predictable, and publishes the Knovum website.

Media and AI requests now carry the model that runs them. When a capability has several ready executors — transcription, alignment, diarization, visual analysis, dubbing and video work, or the four passes of an image derivation — Knovum asks which one to use, with your Settings preference preselected; when exactly one is ready, it runs without interrupting you. That choice is recorded with the request, so retry, restart and resume replay the model you submitted with instead of whatever Settings says today, and each image derivation replays the same model for every pass. A job that was already queued or running keeps its own model through this upgrade; nothing is silently re-routed underneath you.

Audiobook paragraphs and short dubs gain a delivery instruction: up to 500 characters of plain language about pace, tone and how the line should be read. It is saved with the segment and reaches the engine whenever the line is cloned from a reference recording with a voice that accepts a style, and it stays collapsed until you use it. Role bindings now show how many lines each role speaks, and the chapter and dubbing editors hold their layout on narrower windows.

Canvas selection inside groups behaves predictably. Working with nested groups keeps you in the group you were editing, a selection that leaves the group clears that scope instead of pointing at a group that no longer exists, and a plain marquee drag onto empty space deselects — while ⌘/Ctrl-drag or Shift-drag still adds to the selection.

The Knovum website is live at https://madebyzan.github.io/knovum/, in English and Chinese, with product, privacy and download pages, direct per-platform installers and guides for the workflows the desktop app ships.

Knovum now counts anonymous usage. The feature is on by default and sends at most one app-start event per UTC day containing a random installation ID, the app version and the platform. It never includes your Spaces, files, notes, searches, prompts or outputs, no IP address is written to the analytics database, and daily rows are deleted after 90 days. Turn it off at any time in Settings → About: the choice is remembered across restarts, and switching it off clears the local installation ID.
