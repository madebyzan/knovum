05GOOD TO KNOW

A few things, clearly answered.

Still curious? The project documentation is open to everyone.

Read the documentation↗
Does Knovum work offline?

Yes. Editing, organizing, keyword and semantic search, transcription, speaker recognition, OCR, speech synthesis, and the listed on-device image tools run on your machine once their models are downloaded. Cloud-model actions need an internet connection.

Do I need an AI account?

No account is needed to use Knovum or its local capabilities. AI answers and generation require a model connection: a cloud provider, a compatible local endpoint, or a desktop Agent.

Where is my data stored?

Your library lives in local application data. Knovum does not operate a cloud service or upload your library. When you deliberately use a cloud model, the context required for that task is sent to the provider you selected.

What can I bring into a Space?

Notes, webpages, YouTube and other supported video links, recordings, PDFs, Office documents, images, code, and ordinary files. You can also import and export canvases in the open JSON Canvas format.

Is Knovum free and open source?

Yes. Knovum is free and open-source software under the MIT License. You can use、inspect、modify、and distribute it under the terms of that license.
