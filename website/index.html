A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Good to know.

A little more about how Knovum works.

Does Knovum require an account?

No. Knovum does not require an account and does not operate a Knovum cloud service. Your library is stored in local application data on your device.

Can I use Knovum offline?

Yes, for supported local workflows. Editing, organizing, keyword and semantic search, transcription, OCR and listed on-device tools work locally once required models are downloaded. Cloud model tasks need a connection.

Do I need to pay for an AI provider?

No provider is bundled or required for local editing and supported on-device capabilities. AI answers and generation can use a provider you choose, a compatible local endpoint such as Ollama, or a desktop Agent. Provider fees may apply under their own terms.

What platforms are supported?

Knovum currently supports macOS 14 or later (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows 10 version 2004 or later (x64). Linux and Windows on ARM are not supported yet.

Is Knovum free and open source?

Yes. Knovum is free and open-source software released under the MIT License. You can read, build, modify and distribute it under the license terms.
