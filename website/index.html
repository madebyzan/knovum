02A NATURAL WAY OF WORKING

From scattered sources to considered work.

A simple rhythm for research, learning, and creation.

01

Collect without overthinking.

Save links, add files, capture ideas, or bring in a recording. Everything has a place to land.

02

03

03BUILT AROUND TRUST

YOUR KNOWLEDGE IS PERSONAL.

Your library.
Your machine.

Knovum does not require an account and does not run a Knovum cloud service. Your Spaces, files, extracted text, search indexes, and backups live in local application data.

Read how privacy works↗
⌂

Local by design

Your knowledge library and search index are stored on your machine.

◉

Useful without a model account

Editing, organization, local search, transcription, OCR, and other supported on-device capabilities do not require a cloud AI account.

↗

Cloud use stays explicit

When you choose a cloud model, only the context needed for that task is sent to the provider you selected.
