THE IDEA BEHIND KNOVUM

Knowledge is something you build, not just store.

Knovum is a local-first desktop workspace for gathering, exploring, and creating with your own knowledge. It brings the source material and the thinking that grows from it into one place.

Explore the open-source project↗
ABOUT / 01

Thoughtful. Capable. Calm. Open.

A tool should help you think more clearly, not ask for your attention.
01WHY WE'RE BUILDING THIS

The tools we use should respect the work we put into knowing.

Research and creative work rarely happen in a straight line. Sources arrive from different places and in different formats. Ideas change as evidence accumulates. Useful outputs need to retain their connection to the material that shaped them.

Knovum is an attempt to give that process a durable home — one that stays under your control and can grow with the questions you care about.

02WHAT WE BELIEVE

A few principles shape every decision.

01

Knowledge first

AI is a capability, not the product's identity. Start from the material and the outcome a person is trying to reach.

02

Context stays visible

Sources, revisions, and relationships should be inspectable. A result should not become detached from how it came to be.

03

Your data, your ground

The knowledge library is local-first. External model use is a choice with a clear provider boundary.

04

Open by nature

Knovum is MIT-licensed open-source software. Formats and integrations should help your knowledge remain portable.

03UNDER THE SURFACE

A native desktop app, built for serious work.

Knovum combines a native Rust core with a Vue-based interface. Local storage, search, media processing, and model runtimes are designed to work together without requiring a hosted Knovum service.
