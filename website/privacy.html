LOCAL-FIRST, BY DESIGN

Your knowledge should stay yours.

Knovum is built around a simple boundary: your library lives on your device. When you choose to use a cloud model, only the context needed for that task is sent to the provider you selected.

Understand the data boundary↓
PRIVACY / 01

Your library. Your device. Your decision.

Local-first does not mean every optional service is offline. The boundary is explicit.
01THE DATA BOUNDARY

No Knovum account. No Knovum cloud.

Knovum does not require an account and does not operate a hosted knowledge service. Your Spaces, source files, extracted text, search indexes, and backups are stored in local application data on your device.

That means Knovum itself is not a place where your library is uploaded and synchronized. It also means you are responsible for protecting and backing up the device where your library lives.

02WHERE THINGS HAPPEN

Local is the home. Cloud is an explicit choice.

01 / ON YOUR DEVICE
⌂

Your knowledge library

Spaces, files, extracted text, and managed assets stay in local application data. SQLite stores structure; LanceDB supports semantic search.

LOCAL STORAGE
02 / ON YOUR DEVICE
◉

Supported local capabilities

Editing, keyword and semantic search, transcription, speaker recognition, OCR, speech synthesis, and listed image tools can run locally after model downloads.

LOCAL PROCESSING
03 / WHEN YOU CHOOSE
↗

A provider you select

For a cloud-model answer or generation task, Knovum sends the selection and context needed for that task to the provider you chose. That provider's terms apply.

TASK-SCOPED TRANSFER

A cloud provider's handling of requests is governed by that provider's own terms, privacy policy, and account settings.

03WHAT STAYS LOCAL

Your library is not a training feed.

Knovum's architecture keeps the knowledge store on your device. Local application data includes the Space structure, managed content, extracted text, semantic index, and backups.

01
Library data

SQLite, LanceDB, and local managed files.

02
Provider credentials

API keys are stored in a dedicated local secrets database and are not written to logs.

03
Model files

Downloaded when a feature needs them, verified against pinned SHA-256 digests, and removable from Settings.

04A CLEAR-EYED APPROACH

Local-first is a design choice, not a blanket promise.

Some capabilities are local after their models are downloaded. AI answers and generation can use cloud services when you configure and choose them. Knovum makes that distinction explicit; it does not make third-party provider policies on your behalf.

THE PRINCIPLE

Keep your library close. Make every external handoff intentional.
